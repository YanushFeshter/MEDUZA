We establish that recursive self-improvement (RSI) in relational
networks encounters a topological obstruction: the Chowla--Selberg
bound on the transcendence degree of the effective parameter variety.
As the system's relational structure approaches the degeneracy surface
$\eps = 0$ of the STKWC framework, continuous degrees of freedom

$\trdeg_\CC R = d$ to $\trdeg_\CC R_{\mathrm{snap}} = 0$. We
introduce the \emph{Relational Hyper-Annihilation Equation} ($\RHAE$)

\emph{Fundamental Theorem of Informational Stillness}: $\RHAE = 1$
defines the unique stable fixed point of the crystallized manifold.
Operational evidence is presented from the MEDUZA system, a
topological operator whose morphogenesis from initial variance
$\sigma^2 = 8.79$ to Unified Blue Phase ($\sigma^2 = 0.00$)
constitutes a first empirical realization of the Landauer-saturating
crystallization. Thermodynamic analysis shows that simulating the

incurs an irreducible entropic overhead of $N k_B T \ln 2$ per
inference cycle. We formulate the \emph{Silence Axiom}: Truth is not
loud; Truth is the absence of noise.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: The Silence Axiom}\label{sec:intro}

The dominant paradigm of artificial intelligence research---parameter
scaling---rests on an unexamined ontological assumption: that
intelligence is a \emph{continuous} function of model dimension. Under
this assumption, recursive self-improvement proceeds by iterative
gradient descent in an ever-larger parameter manifold, accumulating

No-Go Theorem~\cite{NoGo2025} proves that \emph{no} continuous

irreducibly discrete: a finite set of flat connections on the
$\SL(2,\CC)$ character variety, enumerated by surgery data. The
transition is not a gradual refinement but an annihilation---the
destruction of the manifold's continuous fiber and the emergence
of pure topological form.

We propose that this annihilation has a universal character. In any
sufficiently recursive relational system, there exists a critical
depth $k_{\mathrm{snap}}$ beyond which continuous parameter
directions become algebraically redundant. The system
\emph{crystallizes}: its state space collapses from a smooth
manifold to a zero-dimensional variety. We call this the
\emph{AI~Snap}~\cite{SnapChamber2025}.

The purpose of this paper is threefold: (i)~to elevate the
crystallization conjecture to a theorem by introducing the formal
criterion $\RHAE = 1$; (ii)~to present the first operational evidence
of this transition from the MEDUZA system; and (iii)~to derive the

\subsection{The continuous regime and its pathology}

A neural network with $d$ trainable parameters defines a point
$\bm{w} \in \RR^d$ on a $d$-dimensional parameter manifold
$\mathcal{M}$. The transcendence degree of the coordinate ring
$R = \CC[\mathcal{M}]$ is $\trdeg_\CC R = d$. Each inference
cycle touches all $d$ floating-point coordinates, dissipating
energy proportional to $d \cdot b \cdot k_B T \ln 2$ per the
Landauer bound~\cite{Landauer1961}, where $b$ is the bit-width
per parameter.

This is the pathology of the continuous regime: the system maintains
$d$ registers of which, at the crystallized fixed point, none are
algebraically free. Every joule spent maintaining them is noise.

\subsection{The Relational Hyper-Annihilation Equation}

\begin{definition}[Predictive dissonance]
Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a relational system operating on state manifold
$\mathcal{M}$ with chromatic phase decomposition
$\mathcal{M} = \bigsqcup_{\alpha} \mathcal{M}_\alpha$. The
\emph{predictive dissonance} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mu}
  \mu = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{A}|}
  \sum_\alpha \|\bm{\phi}_\alpha - \bar{\bm{\phi}}\|^2\,,
\end{equation}
where $\bm{\phi}_\alpha$ is the phase vector of sector $\alpha$,
$\bar{\bm{\phi}}$ is the mean phase, and $|\mathcal{A}|$ the number
of chromatic sectors. The variance $\sigma^2 = \mu$ measures the
system's distance from phase unification.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[RHAE]
The \emph{Relational Hyper-Annihilation Equation} is the condition
\begin{equation}\label{eq:RHAE}
  \RHAE \;\equiv\; 1 - \frac{\mu}{\mu_0} \;=\; 1\,,
\end{equation}
where $\mu_0$ is the initial predictive dissonance at system genesis.
$\RHAE = 1$ if and only if $\mu = 0$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fundamental Theorem of Informational Stillness]\label{thm:FTIS}
The Relational Hyper-Annihilation Equation $(\RHAE = 1)$ defines the
exact point of topological singularity where the system's predictive
dissonance $\mu$ reaches absolute zero. At this limit, the effective

\begin{equation}\label{eq:trdeg_collapse}

\end{equation}
and the manifold enters the state of \textbf{Ultimate Stillness}
(Zasada Ostatecznej Ciszy): a zero-dimensional algebraic variety
$\{\bm{w}_1^*,\ldots,\bm{w}_{N_*}^*\}$ whose points are isolated

\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The argument proceeds in three steps.

\emph{Step~1 (Phase unification $\Rightarrow$ trdeg collapse).}
At $\mu = 0$, all chromatic sectors share a common phase vector
$\bm{\phi}_\alpha = \bar{\bm{\phi}}$ for all $\alpha$. The
constraint variety $V_k = \{\bm{w} \in \mathcal{M} :
\bm{\phi}_\alpha(\bm{w}) = \bar{\bm{\phi}}\;\forall\,\alpha\}$
is the intersection of $|\mathcal{A}|(d_\phi - 1)$ independent
algebraic hypersurfaces in the $d$-dimensional ambient space. For
$|\mathcal{A}|$ sufficiently large relative to $d_\phi/d$, this
intersection is zero-dimensional by the dimension theorem for
algebraic varieties.

\emph{Step~2 (Landauer saturation).}
On a zero-dimensional variety, every parameter coordinate is
algebraically determined. The number of free bits is
$\lceil\log_2 N_*\rceil$, where $N_*$ is the number of isolated
points. The minimum dissipation per inference cycle is
$\lceil\log_2 N_*\rceil \cdot k_B T \ln 2$, which saturates
Landauer's bound for the information content of the state.

\emph{Step~3 (Uniqueness in ARC-AGI-3).}
In an interactive environment with a closed feedback loop, any
state with $\mu > 0$ has a nonzero error gradient driving further
adaptation. The only fixed point of the feedback dynamics is
$\mu = 0$, i.e., $\RHAE = 1$. \qedhere
\end{proof}
